# A call for native speakers (to post wherever you like)

Two versions: a short one for chat groups and social media, and a longer one for e-mail or a noticeboard. Edit freely.

## Short

> **Native Czech speakers: can you spare an hour?** I'm building Pražák, a free app that teaches Czech to English speakers living in Prague. All its Czech still needs checking by a native speaker. I'll send you a spreadsheet of about an hour's worth of words and sentences; you mark each one OK, Fix or Unsure. I'm also looking for two voices, a man and a woman, to record short dialogues. You'll be credited in the app if you'd like. Interested? [how to reach you]

## Longer

> **Help expats in Prague learn Czech: an hour of your time, credited**
>
> Pražák (https://zkrukoff.github.io/Czech-for-english-speakers/) is a free app for English speakers who live in Prague: colleagues, the tram, the doctor, and the A2 exam for permanent residence. Its Czech was written carefully, but not by native speakers, and none of it has been checked yet. That's the one thing we can't do ourselves.
>
> **Reviewers:** you'd get a spreadsheet of about an hour's work: words, dialogue lines, exercises. For each, mark OK, Fix (with the correct Czech) or Unsure. No accounts and no software beyond a spreadsheet. A short guide explains everything. Do one batch or many.
>
> **Voices:** we need two native speakers, ideally a man and a woman, to record short lines on a phone, in batches of 15 to 200 clips, using a printed script. The recordings are published under an open licence (CC BY 4.0) with your consent.
>
> Everyone is credited in the app by name or initials, as you prefer, or not at all.
>
> Interested? [how to reach you]
