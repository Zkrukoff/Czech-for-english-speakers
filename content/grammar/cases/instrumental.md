## What it's used for

The instrumental answers **kým? čím?** (by whom? with what?).

1. **The tool or means**, with **no preposition**:
   - *Jedu **vlakem**.* I'm going by train.
   - *Platím **kartou**.* I'm paying by card.
   - *Píšu **perem**.* I'm writing with a pen.
2. **"With" = together with**, after **s / se**:
   - *kafe **s mlékem*** coffee with milk
   - *Jdu do kina **s Petrem**.* I'm going to the cinema with Petr.
3. **Location** after *před, za, nad, pod, mezi* (where something is):
   - *Čekám **před kinem**.* I'm waiting in front of the cinema.
   - *Kočka spí **pod stolem**.* The cat is sleeping under the table.
4. **Time ago** with *před*:
   - ***před týdnem*** a week ago, ***před rokem*** a year ago
5. **What someone becomes or is (formally)**, after *stát se* (become) and sometimes *být*:
   - *Chce se stát **lékařem**.* He wants to become a doctor.

## Prepositions that take the instrumental

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **s / se** | with (together) | ***s mámou**, **se sestrou*** |
| **před** | in front of; before; ago | ***před domem**, **před obědem**, **před hodinou*** |
| **za** (location) | behind; beyond | ***za rohem*** around the corner |
| **nad** | above | ***nad městem*** |
| **pod** | under, below | ***pod mostem*** |
| **mezi** | between, among | ***mezi stolem a oknem*** |

Remember that *před, za, nad, pod, mezi* take the **accusative** when there's movement *to* a place: *Polož to pod stůl* (accusative) → *Je to pod stolem* (instrumental). See the [accusative](/grammar/accusative) page.

## Compared with English

English uses "with" for two different ideas, and Czech separates them:

- **with = using** → bare instrumental: *Jím **lžící**.* I eat with a spoon.
- **with = together with** → *s* + instrumental: *Jím **s kamarádem**.* I'm eating with a friend.

Similarly, English "by train / by car" is the bare instrumental: *jet **autem**, jet **autobusem***.

## Common mistakes

- ***Jedu s autem*** → ***Jedu autem.*** *S autem* means you're travelling together with a car, as if it were a friend.
- ***kafe s mléko*** → ***kafe s mlékem.***
- ***se*** **before hard clusters**: *s* becomes *se* before *s, z, š, ž* and some groups: ***se sestrou**, **se ženou**, **se mnou***.
- **Location vs. movement**: ***pod stolem*** (it's there) vs. ***pod stůl*** (put it there).
