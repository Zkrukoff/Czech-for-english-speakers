## What it's used for

The locative answers **(o) kom? (o) čem?** (about whom? about what?). It is the only case that **always** comes after a preposition.

1. **Location: where something is** (with *v* or *na*):
   - *Bydlím **v Praze**.* I live in Prague.
   - *Jsem **na nádraží**.* I'm at the station.
2. **Talking or thinking about something** (with *o*):
   - *Mluvíme **o filmu**.* We're talking about the film.
3. **After, around** (with *po*):
   - ***po obědě*** after lunch, *procházka **po městě*** a walk around the town

## Prepositions that take the locative

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **v / ve** | in | ***v Brně**, **ve škole**, **v kavárně*** |
| **na** (location) | on, at | ***na stole**, **na poště**, **na univerzitě*** |
| **o** | about | *kniha **o Praze*** |
| **po** | after; around, along | ***po práci**, **po městě*** |
| **při** | during; by, near | ***při jídle**, **při práci*** |

### v or na?

Most places take **v** (in): *v obchodě, v restauraci, v kině, v Česku*. Some take **na**, and these must be learned as a list:

- open spaces and surfaces: *na ulici, na náměstí, na zahradě, na stole*
- many institutions and events: *na poště, na nádraží, na úřadě, na univerzitě, na letišti, na koncertě*
- islands and some regions: *na Moravě, na Slovensku*

The same choice carries over to "where to?": *v kině → do kina*, but *na poště → na poštu* (accusative).

## Sound changes before -ě

Before the ending *-ě*, some consonants change. You'll see this constantly with place names:

| Nominative | Locative |
|---|---|
| Pra**h**a | v Pra**z**e |
| A**m**erika | v Ameri**c**e |
| ře**k**a | na ře**c**e |
| kni**h**a | v kni**z**e |
| sest**r**a | o sest**ř**e |

(*Brno → v Brně*, *Plzeň → v Plzni*, *Londýn → v Londýně*)

## Compared with English

English uses *in, on, at, about* with no change to the noun. Czech uses similar prepositions, but the noun after them takes the locative ending. The trick is to learn the preposition and ending together as a chunk: *v Praze, na poště, o tom*.

## Common mistakes

- ***v Praha*** → ***v Praze.*** Always change the ending after *v, na, o*.
- ***Jsem na poštu*** → ***Jsem na poště.*** Locative for **where**; accusative for **where to**.
- ***v pošta / v nádraží*** → ***na poště / na nádraží.*** Learn the *na* places.
- **Masculine -ě or -u**: *v obchodě, na hradě*, but *v hotelu, v parku, ve vlaku*. After *k, h, ch, g* it's almost always *-u*.
