## What it's used for

The dative answers **komu? čemu?** (to whom? to what?).

1. **The receiver of something** (English "to someone"):
   - *Dám to **mámě**.* I'll give it to Mum.
   - *Napiš **Petrovi**.* Write to Petr.
2. **After verbs that take the dative** (memorise these):
   - *pomáhat / pomoct* (help): *Pomůžu **ti**.* I'll help you.
   - *rozumět* (understand): *Nerozumím **vám**.* I don't understand you.
   - *děkovat / poděkovat* (thank): *Děkuju **ti**.*
   - *věřit* (believe): *Věřím **mu**.*
   - *volat / zavolat* (call): *Zavolám **Janě**.*
3. **Liking**: in *líbit se* the thing liked is the subject, and the person who likes it is in the dative:
   - *Líbí se **mi** Praha.* I like Prague (literally: Prague pleases me).
4. **Feelings and age**, with *je* + dative:
   - *Je **mi** zima.* I'm cold. *Je **mi** líto.* I'm sorry.
   - *Je **mi** třicet let.* I'm thirty.

## Prepositions that take the dative

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **k / ke** | to, towards (a person or object) | *Jdu **k doktorovi**. Pojď **ke mně**.* |
| **kvůli** | because of | ***kvůli dešti*** because of the rain |
| **díky** | thanks to | ***díky tobě*** thanks to you |
| **proti** | against; opposite | ***proti větru*** against the wind |
| **naproti** | opposite | ***naproti nádraží*** |

**k vs. do:** use *do* for going **into** a place, *k* for going **to** a person or up to something: *jdu do nemocnice* (into the hospital), but *jdu k doktorovi* (to the doctor's).

## Compared with English

English often hides the dative in word order: "I gave **Mum** the book" = "I gave the book **to Mum**". Czech marks Mum with the dative ending, so *Dal jsem mámě knihu* and *Knihu jsem dal mámě* both work.

The biggest difference is *líbit se*. English "I like it" has *I* as subject; Czech "*Líbí se mi to*" has *it* as subject and *me* in the dative.

## Common mistakes

- ***Já líbím…*** → ***Líbí se mi…*** Turn the sentence around: the liked thing is the subject.
- ***Pomáhám tě*** → ***Pomáhám ti.*** *Pomáhat* takes the dative, not the accusative.
- ***Rozumím tě*** → ***Rozumím ti.***
- **Titles and names.** With a title and a surname, the title takes *-u* and the surname *-ovi*: *Napíšu **panu Novákovi**.* (I'll write to Mr Novák.)
