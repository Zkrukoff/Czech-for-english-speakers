## What it's used for

The vocative is used **only when you speak directly to someone**: greeting them, calling them, or writing to them.

- *Ahoj, **Petře**!* Hi, Petr!
- *Dobrý den, **pane Nováku**.* Hello, Mr Novák.
- *Děkuju, **mami**.* Thanks, Mum.
- *Milá **Jano**, …* Dear Jana, … (in a letter or e-mail)

It has no prepositions and no other jobs. It's simple, but Czechs notice when it's missing: using the nominative to address someone (*Ahoj, Petr!*) sounds foreign at once.

## How to form it

| Type | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Feminine in **-a** | -a → **-o** | Jana → **Jano**, Martina → **Martino**, žena → ženo |
| Masculine, hard consonant | + **-e** | Pavel → **Pavle**, Karel → **Karle**, pan → **pane** |
| Masculine in **-r** after a consonant | -r → **-ře** | Petr → **Petře**, bratr → **bratře** |
| Masculine in **-k, -h, -ch** | + **-u** | Marek → **Marku**, Novák → **Nováku**, kluk → **kluku** |
| Masculine, soft consonant | + **-i** | Tomáš → **Tomáši**, Ondřej → **Ondřeji**, muž → **muži** |
| Masculine in **-a** | -a → **-o** | Honza → **Honzo**, kolega → **kolego** |
| Everything else | no change | Jiří, Marie, Lucie, paní |

Notice the vowel changes in some names: *Pav**e**l → Pav**l**e*, *Mar**e**k → Mar**k**u*. The *e* that disappears in other cases (Pavla, Marka) also disappears in the vocative.

## Titles and surnames

Put **every word** in the vocative: *pane doktore, pane profesore, pane Nováku*.

**Women's surnames in *-ová* never change**, and *paní* doesn't change either: *Dobrý den, **paní Nováková**.*

A very common polite form uses the title without the name: *Dobrý den, **pane doktore**.* / *Dobrý den, **paní doktorko**.*

## Family words

*mami, tati, babi, dědo*: these familiar forms are what children and adults actually say: *Mami, kde jsi?*

## Compared with English

English has no special form; you just say the name. The closest thing is the tone of voice. In Czech the ending does it.

## Common mistakes

- ***Ahoj, Petr!*** → ***Ahoj, Petře!***
- ***Dobrý den, paní Novákovo*** → ***paní Nováková.*** *-ová* surnames stay unchanged.
- ***Pane Novák*** → ***Pane Nováku.*** The surname changes too.

> **Heard on the street:** in very casual speech some people use the nominative with men's names (*Ahoj, Petr!*). It's widely considered sloppy, so always use the vocative yourself.
