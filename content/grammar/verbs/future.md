## Which future depends on the aspect

How you make the future depends on whether the verb is **imperfective** or **perfective** (see [aspect](/verbs/aspect)).

### Imperfective: budu + infinitive

```verb být
```

The future forms of *být* (*budu, budeš, bude, budeme, budete, budou*) + an imperfective infinitive:

- *Zítra **budu pracovat**.* I'll be working tomorrow.
- *Co **budeš dělat** o víkendu?* What will you do (be doing) at the weekend?
- ***Budeme** se **učit** každý den.* We'll study every day.

*Budu* on its own is also the future of *být*: *Zítra **budu** doma.* I'll be at home tomorrow.

### Perfective: the present forms

Perfective verbs have no present meaning, so their "present" forms express the future:

- ***Napíšu** ti.* I'll write to you.
- ***Zavolám** zítra.* I'll call tomorrow.
- *Kdy **přijdeš**?* When will you come?

Never combine *budu* with a perfective verb: ~~*budu napsat*~~.

### Verbs of motion: po-

The determinate verbs of motion form the future with the prefix **po-** (*jít* uses **pů-**):

| Verb | Future |
|---|---|
| jít | **půjdu**, půjdeš, půjde… |
| jet | **pojedu**, pojedeš, pojede… |
| letět | **poletím**, poletíš… |
| nést | **ponesu**, poneseš… |
| běžet | **poběžím**, poběžíš… |

- *Zítra **pojedu** do Brna.* I'm going to Brno tomorrow.
- ***Půjdeme** do kina?* Shall we go to the cinema?

## Choosing between them

| You want to say | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I'll be doing something (process, duration, habit) | budu + imperfective | *Budu psát celý den.* |
| I'll do it (once, with a result) | perfective present | *Napíšu to dnes.* |
| I'll go somewhere | po- / pů- form | *Půjdu domů.* |

Like English, Czech also uses the present for scheduled plans: *Zítra **jedu** do Prahy.* I'm going to Prague tomorrow.

## Common mistakes

- ***Budu napsat*** → ***Napíšu.***
- ***Budu jít*** → ***Půjdu.***
- ***Já budu jsem…*** → ***Budu…*** *Budu* already contains the person.
