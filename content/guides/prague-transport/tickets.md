Prices change most years, so check the current list before you rely on these.

- **Children under 15** and **people aged 65 and over** travel free in Prague. From age 6, carry proof of age. *(Prices and free travel checked on pid.cz, 17 November 2026.)*
- **If you commute every day, buy an annual pass** (*roční jízdenka*). It works out at a fraction of the cost of single tickets, and some employers contribute to it.

## Where to buy

1. **The PID Lítačka app** (the easiest): buy single tickets and passes on your phone. A ticket becomes valid one or two minutes after you activate it, so **activate it before you get on**, not when you see an inspector.
2. **Contactless terminals** in many trams and buses: tap your bank card and the ticket is valid straight away.
3. **Ticket machines** (*automat na jízdenky*) in metro stations and at bigger stops; most take cards and coins.
4. **Shops**: newsagents (*trafika*) and some supermarkets sell paper tickets.
5. **Text message (SMS)** tickets work only with a Czech mobile number.

## Validate paper tickets

A paper ticket must be **validated** (stamped with the time) when you start your journey, in the small machine called an ***označovač***:

- in the **metro**, at the entrance to the platforms, **before** you go down the escalators
- in **trams and buses**, inside the vehicle as soon as you board

Insert it in the direction of the arrow, once. App tickets and tickets bought at the on-board contactless terminals don't need validating.

## Ticket inspectors

Inspectors (*přepravní kontrola*, colloquially *revizor*) usually wear ordinary clothes. They'll say:

> ***Dobrý den, přepravní kontrola. Vaše jízdenky, prosím.***
> (Hello, ticket inspection. Your tickets, please.)

A real inspector **shows a metal badge** (*odznak*) and an ID card; you can ask to see it. Without a valid ticket you pay a **surcharge** (*přirážka*) of over a thousand crowns, less if you pay on the spot. You can pay by card, and you're entitled to a receipt (*doklad*). Inspectors may ask for your ID.

## Unwritten rules

- **Let people off first** (*nejdřív vystoupit, pak nastoupit*), then get on.
- **Stand on the right** on escalators (*Stůjte vpravo*); the left is for walking.
- **Give up your seat** to older people, pregnant women and anyone who needs it. Younger people are expected to.
- Take off your rucksack in a crowded carriage.
- Keep your voice and phone quiet, and don't eat smelly food.
- Before your stop, move towards the doors and ask ***Vystupujete?*** (Are you getting off?) or ***Pustíte mě, prosím?*** (Could you let me through?).
