## What you'll hear

### On the metro

| Announcement | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ***Ukončete, prosím, výstup a nástup, dveře se zavírají.*** | Please finish getting on and off, the doors are closing. (You'll hear it every time the doors close.) |
| ***Příští stanice: Muzeum.*** | Next station: Muzeum. |
| ***Přestup na linku C.*** | Change here for line C. |
| ***Konečná stanice. Prosíme, vystupte.*** | Terminal station. Please get off. |

## Station names change their endings

Station and stop names are ordinary Czech nouns, so they **decline**. You'll hear (and should say) them in different forms:

| Name | Where? (*na* + locative) | Where to? |
|---|---|---|
| Můstek | *na Můstku* | *na Můstek* |
| Anděl | *na Andělu* | *na Anděl* |
| Florenc | *na Florenci* | *na Florenc* |
| Muzeum | *na Muzeu* | *na Muzeum* |
| Hlavní nádraží | *na Hlavním nádraží* | *na Hlavní nádraží* |
| Náměstí Míru | *na Náměstí Míru* | *na Náměstí Míru* |

So: ***Vystupte na Můstku.*** Get off at Můstek. ***Jede tahle tramvaj na Anděl?*** Does this tram go to Anděl? Most stations take **na**, even names that would take *v* or *do* as ordinary nouns. See the [locative](/grammar/locative) and [accusative](/grammar/accusative) pages.

A few names to read carefully:

- **I. P. Pavlova** is said *í pé Pavlova* (after the physiologist Ivan Petrovič Pavlov). Use the letter names from the [alphabet](/alphabet).
- **Náměstí Míru** means "Peace Square"; **Hlavní nádraží** means "Main Station".
- **Nádraží Veleslavín** is a metro station, not a railway station, despite the name.
