## Apps and websites

- **PID Lítačka** (official app): tickets, passes and a journey planner in one, in Czech and English.
- **pid.cz**: the official site, with timetables, maps, *výluky* (closures) and the journey planner (*Vyhledat spojení*, "find a connection").
- **IDOS** (idos.cz): the national planner for trains and buses across the country, useful for trips outside Prague.
- General map apps also show PID connections.

## Reading a stop timetable

The timetable at a stop (*jízdní řád*) lists departures by hour:

- the big number on the left is the **hour**, the small numbers after it are the **minutes**
- separate columns for ***Pracovní dny*** (weekdays) and ***Sobota, neděle a svátky*** (Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays)
- the list of stops on the route, with how many minutes it takes to each one
- symbols for request stops and step-free vehicles, explained in a key (*vysvětlivky*)

Electronic boards show the line, the direction (*směr*) and the minutes until departure: ***za 3 min*** = in 3 minutes.

## Closures and diversions

Construction and repairs are frequent, especially in summer. Watch for:

- ***výluka***: a planned closure or diversion, announced on yellow posters at stops and in the app
- ***náhradní doprava***: replacement buses, usually marked **X** with the line number (*X-A* replaces metro line A, for example)
- ***odklon***: a diversion. The tram runs a different route.
- ***mimořádnost***: something unplanned, such as an accident or a breakdown

## Asking for help

Drivers, station staff and other passengers will usually help, especially if you try in Czech:

- ***Promiňte, jede tahle tramvaj na Anděl?*** Excuse me, does this tram go to Anděl?
- ***Na které zastávce mám vystoupit?*** Which stop should I get off at?
- ***Kolik zastávek je to na Muzeum?*** How many stops is it to Muzeum?
- ***Kde si můžu koupit jízdenku?*** Where can I buy a ticket?
- ***Kde je výstup na Václavské náměstí?*** Where's the exit to Wenceslas Square?

All of these are in the [phrasebook](/guides/prague-transport?tab=phrases) with audio.
