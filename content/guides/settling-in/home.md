## Finding a flat

Flats are described by rooms: **2+kk** means two rooms plus a kitchenette (*kuchyňský kout*) in one of them; **2+1** means two rooms plus a separate kitchen. Bathroom and hall aren't counted.

| Czech | English |
|---|---|
| ***nájem*** | rent |
| ***kauce*** | deposit |
| ***nájemní smlouva*** | tenancy agreement |
| ***pronajímatel*** / ***majitel*** | landlord / owner |
| ***zálohy na energie*** | monthly advance payments for utilities, settled once a year |
| ***realitka*** | estate agency (everyday word) |

Useful when you call about an advert:

> *Dobrý den, volám kvůli inzerátu na byt. Je ještě volný?*  Hello, I'm calling about the flat advert. Is it still available?
> *Kolik je nájem včetně poplatků?*  How much is the rent including charges?
> *Kdy bych se mohl / mohla přijít podívat?*  When could I come and see it?

Read the contract before you sign, ideally with someone you trust. This guide doesn't give legal advice.

## When something breaks

> *Nejde topení.*  The heating isn't working.
> *Kape kohoutek.*  The tap is dripping.
> *Mohl by se na to někdo podívat?*  Could someone take a look at it?

## Neighbours

A *Dobrý den* in the stairwell goes a long way. The building's notices (on the board by the entrance) are about cleaning rotas, water shut-offs and the annual meter readings (*odečet*).
