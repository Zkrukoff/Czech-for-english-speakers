## At an office (úřad)

Expect *vy*, *Dobrý den*, a queue number and paperwork. Many offices now work by appointment (*objednání*), and a number on the screen tells you when it's your turn.

| Czech | English |
|---|---|
| ***úřad*** | office, authority |
| ***formulář*** | form |
| ***doklad*** | document, ID |
| ***razítko*** | stamp (still very much alive) |
| ***pořadové číslo*** | queue number |
| ***přepážka*** | counter, window |

> *Dobrý den, mám objednaný termín na deset.*  Hello, I have an appointment at ten.
> *Jaké doklady potřebuju?*  Which documents do I need?
> *Můžete mi to napsat?*  Could you write it down for me?
> *Mluvíte anglicky?*  Do you speak English?

## Residence and permits

What you need depends on your citizenship and situation, and the rules change. This guide doesn't give legal or immigration advice: start from the Ministry of the Interior's pages for foreigners (linked under *Sources*), and ask your employer's HR.
