Football (***fotbal***) has the bigger rivalries and the louder stands. The top league is the ***Chance Liga***. *(Seznam Zprávy, 2025.)* Prague has several clubs in it; the two big ones have hated each other, theatrically and sometimes literally, for more than a century. Their match is the ***pražské derby***.

## Sparta

**AC Sparta Praha** plays at ***epet ARENA*** in Letná, Praha 7, its home since 1917, with room for 18,325 people. The nearest metro stations are ***Hradčanská*** (line A) and ***Vltavská*** (line C), and the tram stop is simply called ***Sparta***. *(sparta.cz, checked 18 Nov 2026.)* Fans: *sparťané*.

## Slavia

**SK Slavia Praha** plays at ***Fortuna Arena*** in Vršovice, U Slavie 2a, Praha 10. Until July 2022 it was called *Sinobo Aréna*, and many people just say ***Eden***. *(Prague City Tourism.)*

Slavia's colours, red and white, were agreed at the club's first meeting in 1895: *červená jako symbol srdce a bílá evokující čistotu sportovní myšlenky*, "red for the heart and white for the purity of the sporting idea". The shirts are sewn from a red and a white half, which gives the fans their nickname, ***sešívaní*** ("the stitched-together ones"). *(ČT24.)* Fans: *slávisté*.

> *Komu fandíš?*  Who do you support?
> *Fandím Slavii.*  I support Slavia. (*fandit* + dative)
> *Fandím Spartě.*  I support Sparta.
> *Jsi sparťan, nebo slávista?*  Sparta or Slavia?

That last question is not small talk. Answer carefully, or say *Fandím Bohemce* (Bohemians, the third Prague club) and watch both sides relax.
