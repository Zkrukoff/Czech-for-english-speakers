Hockey (***hokej***, officially *lední hokej*) is the sport that empties offices in May. If you only learn one sport's vocabulary in Czech, make it this one.

## The league

The top league is the ***Tipsport extraliga*** (*extraliga* for short). The 2026/27 season starts on 16 September 2026; the regular season (*základní část*) is followed by the play-offs (*play-off*), and the new champion is known by the end of April. The champions are **Dynamo Pardubice**. *(ČTK, 2 June 2026.)*

In Prague, **HC Sparta Praha** plays its home games at **O2 arena**, Českomoravská 2345/17a, Praha 9: get off at ***Českomoravská*** on metro line B. Tickets are sold through Ticketmaster and Ticketportal, and holders of the Prague Visitor Pass get into regular-season home games free. *(Prague City Tourism, 2026–27 season.)*

> *Jdeme na hokej.*  We're going to the hockey.
> *Kdo dneska hraje?*  Who's playing today?
> *Sparta hraje s Pardubicemi.*  Sparta are playing Pardubice. (*s* + instrumental)

## The national team

In May 2024 Czechia won the World Championship on home ice: **2–0 against Switzerland** in the final in Prague, the first world title since 2010 and the first ever won at home. *(The Hockey News; Daily Faceoff, 26 May 2024.)* The next day thousands of people welcomed the team on **Old Town Square** (*Staroměstské náměstí*), chanting *Kdo neskáče, není Čech!* *(ČTK, 27 May 2024.)*

Big games are watched together: in pubs, in fan zones (*fanzóna*), and on any office screen that can be quietly turned towards the kitchen.

> *Kolik je?*  What's the score?
> *Dva jedna pro nás.*  Two–one to us.
> *Gól!*  Goal!
> *Jdou do prodloužení.*  It's going to overtime.
