Every chant here was found in Czech media, with the date. The rival ones are included because you **will** hear them: knowing what they mean is useful. Joining in is another matter, and the last section says why.

## For the national team

**Kdo neskáče, není Čech!** *Hop, hop, hop!*
"Whoever isn't jumping isn't Czech!" Everyone jumps. The classic, at hockey and football, at home and abroad, and on Old Town Square when the team comes home with a medal. *(ČTK, May 2024; CNN Prima News, June 2021.)*

**Češi, do toho!**
"Come on, Czechs!" (literally "Czechs, into it!"). *Do toho!* on its own works for anyone. *(CNN Prima News, June 2021.)*

## For your club

The national chant comes in club versions: swap *Čech* for your fans' name.

**Kdo neskáče, není sparťan!** and **Sparta, do toho!**
Sparta's versions, remembered fondly by a former Sparta striker years later. *(eFotbal.)*

**Slavia, do toho!**
Slavia's. *(iSport, September 2026.)*

**Kdo neskáče, není Tesla, hop, hop, hop!**
Hockey in Pardubice, at the 2026 title celebrations: *Tesla* is a name from the club's past that fans still use. *(iSport, April 2026.)*

**Par-Par-Pardubice!**
Pardubice's answer to *Sparta, Sparta!* on the station platform before a final. *(Aktuálně.cz, 2007.)*

## For the other lot

**Pardubice u Hradce!**
"Pardubice, near Hradec!": the worst thing you can say about a town is that it's a suburb of its rival, and Pardubice and Hradec Králové are two east Bohemian cities with a famous rivalry. Sparta fans on their way to a final in Pardubice preferred it to swearing. *(Aktuálně.cz, 2007.)*

**Smrt Spartě!**
"Death to Sparta!" Slavia fans' traditional chant about Sparta, heard in Eden even when Sparta isn't playing. *(PrahaIN, October 2022.)* It insults a club, not people, but the league still punishes it: in October 2025 its disciplinary commission (*disciplinární komise*) fined a Slavia assistant coach 30,000 Kč for derogatory chanting after a derby, when he apparently joined in with *smrt Spartě*. *(Deník, 27 October 2025.)*

## What not to repeat

**Jude Slavie** ("Jew Slavia"), from part of Sparta's support, is a different thing. The head of the Federation of Jewish Communities calls it plainly antisemitic, and when Sparta's *kotel* sang it in 2022, the rest of the stadium whistled it down; many Sparta fans call it a disgrace. *(PrahaIN, October 2022.)* You need to recognise it, not sing it. The same goes for anything aimed at a group of people rather than a club: if the sector around you switches to something you don't understand, it's fine to just hold your beer.
