## Tickets

Buy from the club: their websites and apps, or the box office (*pokladna*) on match day. For HC Sparta at O2 arena, the sellers are Ticketmaster and Ticketportal. *(Prague City Tourism.)* A season ticket is a ***permanentka***, a ticket is a ***vstupenka*** (or *lístek*).

## Getting there

Public transport, every time. The stop names are in the football and hockey sections; check the lines on pid.cz on the day. A match-day tram is the best Czech listening practice you'll get all week.

## In the stadium

*Not from an official source: general advice, flagged for review.*

- The loudest stand, behind one goal, is the ***kotel*** ("cauldron"). It stands, sings for ninety minutes and doesn't need your opinion on the referee. Sit elsewhere for your first game.
- Big games, the derby above all, sell out: buy early.
- Wear neutral colours anywhere near the away fans.
- Beer comes in plastic cups and is part of the ritual. At hockey, the breaks between periods (*přestávka*) are for the queue.
- Whistling (*pískot*) is booing.

> *Dvě vstupenky na sobotu, prosím.*  Two tickets for Saturday, please.
> *Kde je sektor B?*  Where's section B?
> *Dáme si pivo o přestávce?*  Shall we get a beer at the break?
