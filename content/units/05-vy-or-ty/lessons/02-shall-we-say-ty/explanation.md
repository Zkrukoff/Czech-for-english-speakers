## The offer

Switching from *vy* to *ty* is a small ritual. Someone asks:

> **Můžeme si tykat?** Shall we say *ty* to each other?

and usually gives their first name straight away: *Já jsem Petra.*

## Who offers

Traditionally the **older** or **more senior** person offers, and between a man and a woman of about the same age, the woman. At a young office, people your age may say *ty* from day one. Then just follow their lead.

## How to accept

> **Jasně, rád.** (a man) / **Jasně, ráda.** (a woman) … *Já jsem Tom.*

Over a drink, the switch is often sealed with a toast: ***Na zdraví!*** (look each other in the eye). From then on, *ty* for good: going back to *vy* would feel cold.

## After the switch

Everything changes with it: *Jak se **máš**?, od **tebe**, Petro* instead of *paní Kovářová*. If you slip back into *vy* now and then, people will laugh it off.
