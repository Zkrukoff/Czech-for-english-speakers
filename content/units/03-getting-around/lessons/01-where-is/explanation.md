## Asking the way

- ***Promiňte, kde je** nádraží?* Excuse me, where's the station?
- ***Kde je tady** nejbližší lékárna?* Where's the nearest pharmacy (around here)?
- ***Je to daleko?*** Is it far?

*Promiňte* is the polite "excuse me" for strangers; *promiň* is for friends.

## Understanding directions

Directions are usually given with the **vy imperative** of *jít* (go):

| Czech | English |
|---|---|
| **Jděte rovně.** | Go straight on. |
| **Jděte doleva / doprava.** | Go left / right. |
| **Zahněte doleva.** | Turn left. |
| **Pak…** | Then… |
| **Je to tady / tam.** | It's here / there. |
| **Je to blízko / daleko.** | It's near / far. |
| **Je to asi pět minut pěšky.** | It's about five minutes on foot. |

## Landmarks: vedle, blízko, naproti

Words for position take a case. The most common ones take the **genitive**:

| | |
|---|---|
| **vedle** banky | next to the bank |
| **blízko** nádraží | near the station |
| **u** náměstí | by the square |
| **naproti** poště | opposite the post office (*naproti* takes the dative!) |

Genitive singular endings you've seen so far: feminine *-a → -y* (*banka → vedle banky*), neuter *-o → -a* (*kino → vedle kina*), and *-í* nouns don't change (*nádraží, náměstí*).

## Saying thank you

*Děkuju moc.* Thank you very much. → ***Není zač.*** / ***Rádo se stalo.*** You're welcome.
