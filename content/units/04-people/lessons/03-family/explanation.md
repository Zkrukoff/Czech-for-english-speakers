> **Careful:** *přítel / přítelkyně* usually means a romantic partner. For a friend, say *kamarád / kamarádka*. "To je moje přítelkyně" tells everyone she's your girlfriend.

## I have…: mít + accusative

*Mám **bratra** a **sestru**.* I have a brother and a sister. The accusative of masculine **animate** nouns ends in **-a** (*bratr → bratra, syn → syna*), and feminine **-a** becomes **-u** (*sestra → sestru*). Soft feminines change **-e → -i**: *přítelkyně → mám přítelkyni*.

## How old? The dative

Czech doesn't say "I am thirty". It says "to me it is thirty (years)":

- ***Kolik ti je?*** How old are you? (formal: *Kolik vám je?*)
- ***Je mi*** *třicet (let).* I'm thirty.
- ***Synovi je*** *sedm.* My son is seven. ***Dceři je*** *čtyři.* My daughter is four.
- ***Kolik jim je?*** How old are they?

The word for "years" depends on the number: *jeden **rok**, dva **roky**, pět **let***. *Let* is the special genitive plural of *rok*, used from five up.
